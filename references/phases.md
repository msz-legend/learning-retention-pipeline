# Phase scripts

## Decompose
Collect goal, context, time, background, output preference, and explicit mode. Produce 3–5 subskills, dependencies, and observable acceptance criteria. Validate and obtain confirmation.

## Cognitive
Ask the learner to explain or attempt first. Then give a short correction and require another attempt. Record output, feedback, and no more than two key errors.

## Associative
Assign one no-prompt retrieval task. Space sessions, interleave subskills, and vary tool, scene, difficulty, or input. Do not unlock dependent work before the prerequisite threshold.

## Autonomous
Assign one realistic task with an explicit acceptance checklist and optional pressure condition. Only actual output can pass the exit.

## Retention
Check due date, assign a minimum-dose retrieval task, and run a held-out test at least monthly. A decline beyond the locked threshold creates a proposal; user confirmation and held-out validation are both required before adoption.
