# Retention matrix

| Type | Minimum effective dose | Held-out test |
|---|---|---|
| procedural | weekly real use, 20–60 minutes (or user's smaller budget with risk flag) | timed real task |
| knowledge | every 2–4 weeks, no-prompt output | explain or retrieve without notes |
| social | monthly real scenario and review | real interaction debrief |
| hybrid | combine the dominant type doses | mixed transfer task |

MVP spacing: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days. Failure or marked decline moves back one interval. Postponement defaults to one day; after three consecutive postponements ask whether to shorten, reschedule, or pause.
